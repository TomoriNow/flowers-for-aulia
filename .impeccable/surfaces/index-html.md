# Bouquet page (index.html)

Scope: the single page of the site. Mode: Experience. Audience: Aulia, opening a link from Sean on any device. Job: receive the gift and play with it. Content: the bouquet and the fixed message only. Chosen direction: "Made of Her Name" (user-picked from three mockups; the picked sketch is `.impeccable/mocks/decision/made-of-her-name.html`). Memorable moment: realising the flowers are spelled out of the message. Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The bouquet is literally made of the message: every petal, leaf and stem is a letter from "MADE WITH LOVE FOR MY DEAREST AULIA ~ SEAN". Refuses the black terminal with glowing monospace that ASCII pages default to.

OWN-WORLD: One flat deep-wine ground (#2b0a1e); no glow, gradients or shadows. Bold uppercase mono letters as matter: three pinks per bloom (deep, mid, blush), sage greens for stems and leaves. One heavy grotesk (Archivo Black) sets the message in blush, signature in rose. Focus ring and text selection in the same pinks.

STORY: She opens the link; loose letters drift in, gather from the stems up and open into a turning bouquet of roses and tulips; the message appears; she finds she can spin it and scatter it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: No nav, no scroll. Portrait: bouquet centred at about 64% of the height on tablets (about 50 to 57% on phones, where letters are held at a readable 10px minimum), message centred under it, one small hint line. Landscape (7:5 and wider): bouquet left at about 85% of the height, message right at display size. The bouquet is the primary action. Signature interaction: scatter and gather (tap bursts the letters apart, they re-condense and re-bloom); drag or arrow keys turn it. Motion grammar: letters travel as matter with staggered ease-out, rotation is slow and constant, only the message fades, once.

FORM: alphabet storm, a dealt challenger chosen by the user over the assigned direction; seed:3c44283fb418d15f.
